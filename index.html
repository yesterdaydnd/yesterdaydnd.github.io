Channels

A private, local tool its owner uses to manage their own YouTube channels.

What it is

Channels is a desktop app that runs only on its owner's computer. It is not a public service and has no user accounts: the only person who signs in to it is its owner, to connect the YouTube channels they own or manage.

What it does

  • Shows each connected channel's statistics and analytics in one place.
  • Lists the channel's videos and their metadata.
  • In later versions, uploads and schedules the owner's own videos and manages their own playlists and comments, with the owner's consent.

Uses YouTube API Services

Channels uses the YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. See the Channels privacy policy and the Google Privacy Policy.
