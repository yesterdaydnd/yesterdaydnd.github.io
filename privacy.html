Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Channels is a desktop app that runs only on its owner's computer, for managing the YouTube channels its owner connects to it. It uses the YouTube API Services. By using Channels you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data it accesses

With the permissions you grant on Google's consent screen, Channels reads, for each channel you connect:

  • channel details (name, handle, images, statistics);
  • the channel's videos and their metadata;
  • YouTube Analytics reports for the channel (views, watch time, audience, traffic sources and, when the channel is monetized and you grant it, revenue figures).

If you grant the management permission, Channels can also upload, schedule and edit your videos, thumbnails, captions and playlists, and reply to or moderate comments, only when you, or a schedule you set, ask it to.

How it uses and stores the data

  • The data is used only to show you your own channels and to carry out actions you ask for.
  • Everything is stored only on your own computer. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any server other than Google's.
  • Sign-in tokens are encrypted on your computer with Windows data protection.
  • YouTube data is refreshed or deleted at least every 30 days. Statistics are kept only while the channel stays authorized.
  • Channels does not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone, and does not use cookies or tracking on this website.

Removing access and data

  • Disconnect a channel in the app (Settings → Disconnect). This revokes its access and deletes the channel's stored YouTube data.
  • You can also revoke access at any time from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Channels then stops refreshing that channel, and its stored YouTube data is deleted within 30 days. To delete it at once, disconnect the channel in the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy go to the developer contact shown on the app's Google consent screen.
